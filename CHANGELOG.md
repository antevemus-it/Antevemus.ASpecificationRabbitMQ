## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-15

### Added

- `RabbitMqTransport` (implements the core's `IMessageTransport`): `new RabbitMqTransport(RabbitMqConnector $connector, RabbitMqTopology $topology = new RabbitMqTopology(), float $confirmTimeout = 5.0)`. `send()` publishes the batch to the topic exchange of the topology, the routing key being the message name, the body the core's wire form (`Message::toJson()`, `content_type` application/json), persistent (`delivery_mode` 2), with `message_id`, `type` and `timestamp`, and the headers `aspec-tenant` and `aspec-ordering-key` when the message has them. Publisher confirms are always on and awaited once per batch: a `basic.ack` confirms a message (its broker id is the message id); a message returned as unroutable (the `mandatory` flag, `312 NO_ROUTE`) fails even though the broker acknowledges it afterwards; a `basic.nack` fails; a failure of the connection or of the channel, or no answer within the timeout, fails every message not confirmed yet and closes the connection, which the next send reopens. `send()` never throws; no reason carries the payload. `close()`.
- `RabbitMqSource` (implements the core's `IMessageSource`): `new RabbitMqSource(RabbitMqConnector $connector, string $queue, RabbitMqTopology $topology = new RabbitMqTopology(), int $pollIntervalMs = 25, float $confirmTimeout = 5.0)`. `receive($max, $waitMs)` pulls with `basic.get` and manual acknowledgement until it has `$max` deliveries or the queue is empty, polling an empty queue every `pollIntervalMs` until `$waitMs` runs out; the body is read by `ReceivedMessage::decode()` (an invalid body is an invalid delivery, never dropped); the delivery count is `x-delivery-count + 1` (the `redelivered` flag counts as the second delivery when the header is absent), plus the redeliveries the source made itself; the broker id is `<connection>.<delivery tag>`. `ack()` is `basic.ack`. `reject($requeue = true)` keeps the delivery, unacknowledged, and hands it out again before anything newer, in the order the deliveries were first received. `reject($requeue = false)` publishes the body and properties, with a confirm and `mandatory`, to the dead letter exchange, adds `aspec-dead-reason`, `aspec-dead-delivery-count` and `aspec-dead-queue`, and only then acknowledges the original. A failure of the broker closes the connection, forgets what the source held (the broker gives it back) and throws a `MessagingException` of the core naming the queue and the broker. `heldCount()`, `close()`, `queue()`, `DEFAULT_POLL_INTERVAL_MS`.
- `RabbitMqTopology`: a durable topic exchange (`aspec.messages` by default), per consumer a durable quorum queue bound by routing-key patterns with `x-dead-letter-exchange` and `x-dead-letter-routing-key`, a durable direct dead letter exchange (`<exchange>.dead`) and per consumer a quorum dead letter queue `<queue>.dead`. `declare($connector, [queue => patterns])`, `declareExchanges($channel)`, `declareQueue($channel, $queue, $patterns)`, all idempotent; `exchange()`, `deadLetterExchange()`, `deadLetterQueueOf()`; the rules of a name and of a pattern as constants.
- `RabbitMqConnector`: from the host, port, credentials, virtual host, timeouts and heartbeat (an `AMQPStreamConnection` of php-amqplib per connection), `fromUrl('amqp://user:password@host:port/vhost')` or `fromFactory(Closure)` (TLS or any option of php-amqplib); `describe()` names the broker without the credentials, and the password (and the URL) is a `#[SensitiveParameter]`.
- Documentation in `docs/en` and `docs/pt-BR` (Docs Kit format), every PHP block run by the test suite, the ones that talk to a broker against the test broker.

### Notes

- Requires `antevemus/aspecification` `^2.14` and `php-amqplib/php-amqplib` `^3.7` (pure PHP; `ext-sockets` and `ext-mbstring`; the `amqp` PECL extension is not needed); RabbitMQ 3.13 or later. Uses only the public API of the core's messaging hook, and the core is not changed.
- **Quorum queues**, because they count the deliveries (`x-delivery-count`), which gives the consumer a delivery count that survives a dead process and lets it dead-letter a message that keeps killing its worker.
- **The requeue is kept in the source**: measured against RabbitMQ 3.13, a quorum queue puts a message rejected with requeue at the tail of the queue, behind the later messages of its ordering key, which would break the contract of the hook (a requeued delivery comes back before any later delivery of its key). The source keeps it, still unacknowledged; a dead process leaves it to the broker, which gives the deliveries of a closed connection back at the head of the queue, in order. Consequence, declared: the redeliveries a source made itself are not known to the broker, so after a crash the delivery count restarts from the broker's.
- **Pull, not push**: `basic.get`, so the source never holds a message the consumer was not handed, and `basic.qos` does not apply; the bound on the deliveries in flight is the `$max` of `receive()`.
- **Dead letters published by the source**, not by `basic.nack` without requeue, so that they carry the reason in a header; the broker's own dead letters (a delivery limit, a TTL) reach the same queue through the queue arguments.
- PHP `^8.2`; the same suite passes on PHP 8.2.34 and 8.4.27.
- Suite: 168 tests, 1 805 assertions with a broker (113 run and 55 skipped without one): Unit 108, Contract 24 (12 scenarios over the core's in-memory pair and over RabbitMQ, the same transcripts), Broker 30, Property 6 (3 seeds × 150 steps over each pair). Mutation testing over the whole `src/`: 274 mutants, 269 killed, 1 time-out, 4 escaped, all equivalent (listed in `infection.json5`); MSI 98.54%.
- The CI of GitHub goes green only once `antevemus/aspecification` 2.14.0 is on Packagist.
